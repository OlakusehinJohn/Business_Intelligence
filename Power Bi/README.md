# Business Performance Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive sales dashboard created with Microsoft Power Bi.
It shows how much the business sells,how sales come from across Nigeria States, customers activities and sales distribution.
The dashboard helps users understand business performance and make decisions using sales data

## Business Problem
Management need a clear and interactive way to monitor business performance and identify areas that require attention.
The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:
- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were created?
- What is the average order?
- How many unique customers made the purchase?
- Which State is generating the most Order?
- How is revenue changing overtime?
- Which product category is the best?
- Which areas are performing above or below expectation?
- Where should management focus on?

## Project Goals
This project is to transform sales data into clear and useful insights that support better business decisions like:
- Which areas require attention
- What factors are driving perfprmance
- How the business is performance
- How sales change from month to month and year to year
